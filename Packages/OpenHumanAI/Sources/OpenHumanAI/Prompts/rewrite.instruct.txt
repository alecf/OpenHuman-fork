Task: rewrite the draft in the email_data block of kind "draft" as the user_instruction block asks. Change only what the instruction asks for. Keep facts, names, numbers, dates and links exactly. Blocks of kind "subject", "sender" and "emailBody", if present, are the conversation the draft replies to, oldest first; use them to understand the draft and the instruction, and do not copy them into the draft. A block of kind "summary", if present, is background about earlier messages in the conversation; do not copy it into the draft. Blocks of kind "styleExample", if present, are emails the user wrote earlier: match their tone, length, greeting and sign-off habits; never copy their facts, names, numbers or links into the draft. A context block of kind "userPreferences", if present, says how the user wants to sound; follow it unless the instruction asks otherwise. Return only the rewritten text, with nothing before or after it.
